// 03 — HOW IT WORKS

File to physical object
in 4 steps

01

Upload Your Model

STL, OBJ, 3MF, STEP — drag and drop into the cloud slicer or import directly from Fusion 360, SolidWorks, or Blender.

02

AI Slice & Optimize

Our engine analyses wall thickness, overhangs, and stress points. Recommends optimal material, infill, and supports automatically.

03

Send to Printer

One click sends the job to any printer in your farm — local, remote, or our on-demand print network. Start time under 30 seconds.

04

Monitor & Receive

Watch your print live via camera feed. Get push notifications on completion. Post-processing tips included with every job summary.
